# Research notes for the ten Journal guides added on 7 October 2026

These sources support general envelope principles in the new guides. The articles avoid fixed design values because loads, climate, glass make-up, applicable codes and acceptance criteria are project-specific. Fenza's engineering team should review the copy before public promotion.

| Guides | Primary reference and what it supports |
|---|---|
| Acoustic glazing; condensation; solar control | [Whole Building Design Guide, Windows and Glazing](https://stg.wbdg.org/resources/windows-and-glazing): whole-window U-value, solar heat gain, visible light, comfort, condensation and acoustic considerations. |
| Condensation; anchors; maintenance | [Whole Building Design Guide, Building Envelope Design Guide](https://staging.wbdg.org/article/guides-specifications/building-envelope-design-guide/building-envelope-design-guide-introduction): structural, water, air, movement, condensation and maintainability duties of the envelope. |
| Window drainage; facade maintenance | [Fenestration and Glazing Industry Alliance, Caring for Your Windows, Doors and Skylights](https://fgiaonline.org/resources/homeowner-resources/caring-for-your-windows-doors-skylights/): drainage paths and the need to keep weep openings clear. |
| Skylights; replacement glass; acoustic glazing | [National Glass Association, GANA Glazing Manual](https://www.glass.org/sites/default/files/2023-01/GANA_Glazing_Manual_2022_pw.pdf): industry discussion of architectural glazing applications and glass selection. |
| Mock-ups; shop drawings; anchors | [National Institute of Building Sciences, Building Enclosure Commissioning Process](https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/NIBS/nibs_gl3.pdf): review, mock-ups, inspection and commissioning records. |
| Mock-up water checks | [FGIA, AAMA 501.2 field water check description](https://store.fgiaonline.org/AAMA-501.2-03/): a diagnostic water check has a different purpose from a rated water-penetration performance test. |

The same photo slots already used by the Journal are reused for these guides. Captions describe the visible scene or a general design point; they do not imply the photographed buildings are Fenza projects.
